# Code of conduct

Cre8Labs IP Switcher is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly place to ask questions, report problems and contribute.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to networking or to open source.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person, and make it specific and constructive.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information, such as addresses, network details or contact details, without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers through [cre8labs.uk](https://cre8labs.uk). Reports are handled privately.
